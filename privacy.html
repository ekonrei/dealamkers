Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EKON Investments LLC ("Dealmakers," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your information when you visit our website, book a call, or join our mentorship program.

01 Information We Collect

We collect information you give us and information created when you use our website and mentorship program, including:

  • Contact information — name, email, phone number, and anything you share when booking a call or applying.
  • Program information — your goals, market, experience, and the deals, documents and messages you share with us during mentorship.
  • Payment information — billing details, processed by our payment providers. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Usage data — device information, IP address, pages viewed, and how you interact with our site and video.

02 How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Schedule and run strategy calls and mentorship sessions.
  • Deliver course content, templates and program access.
  • Process payments and manage your enrollment.
  • Send program updates, reminders and support responses by email, text or phone.
  • Improve our website, content and program.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

03 Text Messages and Calls

If you give us your phone number, you agree we may contact you by call or text about your booking and the program. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time. We do not share your phone number or text opt-in consent with third parties for their marketing.

04 How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the program, and as required by law:

  • Scheduling tools — when you book a strategy call.
  • Course and community platforms (such as Discord and Whop) — to give you program access.
  • Payment processors — to handle billing securely.
  • CRM and communication tools — to manage follow-up, email and text messages.
  • Analytics and advertising providers — to understand site traffic and measure ads.
  • Legal and compliance — when required by law or to protect our rights and users.

05 Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies, including advertising pixels, to measure traffic and improve your experience. You can control cookies in your browser settings, and see where we load advertising tracking and turn it off on our Privacy & advertising choices page.

06 Data Retention

We keep your information as long as needed to provide the program and for legal, tax and accounting purposes. After that, it is deleted or anonymized.

07 Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information. To make a request, email kaden@ekonrei.com.

08 Children's Privacy

The program is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

09 Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above.
