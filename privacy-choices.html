Privacy & Advertising Choices

Updated November 21, 2026

Applying for the Inner Circle

When you apply, you book a call through Calendly, our scheduling tool. The details you enter there (such as your name, email, phone number and answers) go to Calendly and may be added to our CRM. We use them to review your application, run your strategy call and follow up with you. Calls and texts follow the consent choices shown on the booking form, and you can reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time.

If you join, Whop processes your payment. Whop and its payment providers handle your card details; we never see or store your full card number.

Advertising measurement

Where it's allowed, this page may load the Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) and the Google tag to measure visits that come from our ads and to improve who sees them. Meta and Google may receive standard browser and device information, your IP address, the page address, the time of your visit, basic activity on this page (such as scrolling or clicking Apply), and cookies they've already set. Each company may combine this with information it holds, under its own privacy policy.

This page never has your name, email, phone number or application answers, so it doesn't send them to Meta or Google.

If you arrive from one of our ads, we keep the ad's campaign tags (UTM tags such as source, campaign and ad) for that browser tab and add them to the booking link. That lets us see which ads bring in applications.

Where we don't load it

We don't load advertising tracking when your device's time zone is in Europe (including the EU, the UK and Switzerland), or when it doesn't tell us a region. We also respect Global Privacy Control, Do Not Track and the choice you save below. We don't use a cookie banner.

Your choice

Checking…

Your choice is saved in this browser and takes effect right away, including in other open tabs of this site. Turning tracking off stops future measurement; it doesn't recall information that was already sent. Clearing your browser data also clears your choice. Visits made while tracking was off aren't sent later if you turn it back on.
